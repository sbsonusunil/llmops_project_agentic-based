
2025 AI Engineering Report - Structured Summary
==============================================

Source:
Amplify Partners – The 2025 AI Engineering Report

Overview
--------
The report analyzes trends in AI engineering, LLM adoption, RAG systems,
model deployment, infrastructure, evaluation, and enterprise AI workflows.
It is based on responses from AI engineers, founders, and organizations
building production AI systems.

Key Findings
------------

1. OpenAI Dominance
- OpenAI models dominate customer-facing AI applications.
- 3 of the top 5 most-used models are from OpenAI.
- Half of the top 10 most popular models are OpenAI models.

2. RAG Adoption
- Around 70% of respondents use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  in some form.
- RAG has become a core architecture for enterprise AI applications.
- Companies use RAG to reduce hallucinations and improve factual grounding.

3. Model Update Frequency
- More than 50% of teams update their models at least monthly.
- Around 17% update models weekly.
- Continuous iteration is becoming normal in production AI systems.

4. Evaluation Importance
- Evaluation is now considered a critical component of LLMOps.
- Teams evaluate:
  * correctness
  * hallucination rates
  * retrieval quality
  * latency
  * user satisfaction
- Golden datasets and automated evaluation pipelines are increasingly common.

5. Agentic AI Growth
- AI agents and multi-step workflows are rapidly growing.
- Engineers are using frameworks such as:
  * LangGraph
  * CrewAI
  * AutoGen
  * Semantic Kernel
- Agentic workflows combine reasoning, tool usage, and memory.

6. Rise of Open-Source Models
- Open-source models are improving rapidly.
- Popular models include:
  * Llama
  * Mistral
  * Qwen
  * DeepSeek
- Organizations use open-source models for:
  * privacy
  * cost optimization
  * on-premise deployment

7. Vector Databases
- Vector databases remain foundational for RAG systems.
- Commonly used vector stores:
  * Pinecone
  * Weaviate
  * Chroma
  * Milvus
  * FAISS

8. Infrastructure Trends
- GPU infrastructure demand continues to increase.
- Companies optimize for:
  * inference cost
  * latency
  * throughput
  * scalability
- Quantization and efficient serving frameworks are becoming popular.

9. LLMOps Stack
- Common tools in production:
  * LangChain
  * LangSmith
  * LlamaIndex
  * Weights & Biases
  * vLLM
  * Ollama

10. Enterprise Concerns
- Main concerns include:
  * hallucinations
  * security
  * data privacy
  * prompt injection
  * compliance
- Evaluation and monitoring are essential for production systems.

Major Industry Direction
------------------------
The industry is moving from:
- simple chatbots
toward:
- production AI systems
- autonomous agents
- enterprise copilots
- tool-using AI systems
- multimodal AI applications

Conclusion
----------
The 2025 AI Engineering landscape focuses heavily on:
- reliable AI systems
- evaluation and observability
- agentic workflows
- RAG architectures
- scalable inference
- continuous deployment and monitoring

AI engineering is evolving from experimentation to mature software
engineering practices for production-grade AI systems.
